### 2. Parties Identified
- **Company**: Electric City Corp., a Delaware corporation.
- **Distributor**: Electric City of Illinois LLC.

### 3. Key Terms & Obligations
- **Term**: Initial term of ten (10) years, commencing upon delivery of the last Sample by Company to Distributor.
- **Renewal**: Renewable annually for up to an additional ten (10) years on the same terms and conditions (Section 1.3).
- **Exclusivity**: Company appoints Distributor as its **exclusive** distributor within the Market (State of Illinois) (Section 1.1).
- **Territory**: State of Illinois, including public/private entities, institutions, schools, etc. (Recital C).
- **Financial Commitments**:
  - **Letter of Credit (LC)**: Distributor must issue an irrevocable LC in the amount of $500,000 at the commencement of the Term (Section 2.4(a)).
  - **Minimum Purchase Order**: A minimum of $250,000.00 purchase order must be received by Company by the first of each month for a total 12-month period.
  - **Unit Commitment**: 375 units in the first Product Year (1999).
  - **Price Adjustment**: Prices subject to annual adjustment based on CPI starting calendar year 2000.

### 4. Risk Analysis (Customer/Distributor Perspective)

#### High Severity Risks
- **Automatic Renewal Trap (Section 1.3)**: The agreement is renewable for up to ten additional years on the same terms. There is no specified right of first refusal or termination notice period for renewal.
  - *Redline*: Add a requirement that Distributor must provide written notice at least 90 days prior to each annual renewal date to exercise the option to renew.
- **Indemnification Scope (Section 5.3)**: Mutual indemnification covers breach of material representations, negligence, fault, and IP violations. However, the clause also requires Distributor to indemnify Company for "unauthorized Product alteration" even if Company is not at fault, which could expose Distributor to significant liability for product modifications made without explicit approval.
- **Termination for Cause (Section 4.2)**: Either party may terminate upon 30 days' notice for default. This short cure period may be insufficient for complex breaches.

#### Medium Severity Risks
- **Repurchase Obligation (Section 4.4)**: Upon expiration or termination, Company has the sole discretion to repurchase Products from Distributor at net invoice price less allowances. This lack of guaranteed buyback could leave Distributor with unsold inventory risk.
- **Limitation of Liability (Force Majeure)**: While there is protection for events beyond reasonable control (Acts of God, etc.), the clause does not explicitly cap overall liability, potentially exposing both parties to unlimited damages for non-force majeure breaches.

### 5. Upcoming Obligations & Deadlines
- **Renewal/Non-Renewal Notice**: Due **2026-09-15**. Both parties must send renewal/non-renewal notice before the term end.
- **Quarterly Reports**: Distributor must submit written reports to Company each quarter during the first year of the Term, commencing 90 days after execution (Section 4.1).
