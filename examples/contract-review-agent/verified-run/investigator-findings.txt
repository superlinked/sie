## Contract investigation findings

Document type: Distributor Agreement. Parties: Electric City Corp. (Company) and Electric City of Illinois L.L.C. (Distributor).

No execution dates are visible, so execution is not established from the visible signature page.

Commercial terms: Section 1.1 appoints Distributor as the exclusive distributor in the Illinois Market. Section 1.3 sets a ten-year initial term beginning when Company delivers the last Sample and permits conditional annual one-year renewals for up to ten additional years if Distributor complies with the Agreement. Section 1.6 requires an irrevocable $500,000 letter of credit, a $250,000 purchase order by the first day of each month, and at least 375 units in the first Product Year. Section 4.1 requires quarterly written reports during the first year. Section 6.9 applies Illinois law.

Material risks and redlines:
Section 1.3 | severity: high
Issue: Missing explicit renewal election or notice mechanics.
Suggested redline: Add explicit language requiring Distributor to provide written notice of its election to renew at least 90 days prior to the expiration of the then-current Term.

Section 4.2 | severity: high
Issue: 30 days is the termination-notice period; the separate cure period is an undefined commercially reasonable time. Curable defaults do have that cure period.
Suggested redline: Replace 'commercially reasonable time' with a specific, fixed number of days (e.g., 30 days) for curing any material default after receipt of written notice.

Section 4.4 | severity: medium
Issue: Company generally may, at its option, repurchase; but Company shall repurchase unopened Product when it terminates without cause for reasons other than Distributor's minimum failure. The clear mandatory exception protects that scenario; the risk is inventory exposure after other expirations or terminations, where repurchase remains optional.
Suggested redline: Change 'may, at its option... repurchase' to 'shall repurchase' for all Products in Distributor's possession upon any expiration or termination, regardless of cause.

Section 5.3 | severity: high
Issue: Each party covers its own breach, negligence, and IP violations; the Distributor-specific indemnity applies only when Company is not at fault.
Suggested redline: Remove the 'provided that Company is not at fault' condition from the Distributor’s indemnification obligation to ensure full coverage for claims arising from Distributor’s sales and marketing practices.

Deadline status as of August 10, 2026: June 30, 2026 quarterly compliance attestation is overdue; July 1, 2026 annual subscription or license fee is overdue; September 15, 2026 renewal or non-renewal notice is upcoming.
