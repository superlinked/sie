# Ordinary-name tag policy, prospective v1

The task is to tag a document with named people, organizations and locations. Gold is AI-agent-prepared and must be independently AI-agent-reviewed before outputs. This is a new ordinary-name engineering fit study. The original custom-type and separate-description results remain unchanged.

Input is the exact selected contiguous paragraph. Do not invent a name or expand an acronym. Preserve source spelling, case, accents and internal punctuation. Annotation positions are Python Unicode character offsets, start inclusive and end exclusive. Every occurrence is annotated; the business metric deduplicates literal `(text,type)` pairs so repeated mentions do not increase document weight.

- `person`: a named human, including a surname alias used as a human reference. Exclude titles, anonymous roles, pronouns and a person's name contained in a facility name when the text refers to the facility.
- `organization`: a named agency, company, university, committee or organizational unit. Include full qualified body names and separately written aliases. Generic staff and industry names are excluded. Software, languages, products, reports, awards, programs and event titles do not become organizations merely because they have proper names.
- `location`: a named country, state, city, continent, natural geographic feature or named physical site, including a mine or airport. Exclude unnamed places, generic direction words and names contained inside a longer organization or facility name. A full airport name is one location, without nested city/person tags.

Flat spans do not overlap. A full named organization or site takes precedence over contained names. Honorifics, possessive suffixes and surrounding punctuation are excluded. A parenthetical alias is a separate span when it follows a complete body name; if it is inside one qualified organizational unit, the full unit remains one span. Generic trailing words such as `community` do not extend a body name. A named organization used as a modifier of a separately named program or event may remain an organization mention; an identifier such as ISO/IEC 17043 or an aircraft model is handled as a product/standard rather than a separate organization mention. Boundary/type ambiguities must be reviewed before outputs and described as annotation choices rather than fabricated facts.

Primary display gold splits literal city/state datelines: RESTON plus Va., Jackson plus Mississippi, Millstone plus West Virginia. Each of those three complete source strings may also be returned as a single location under an explicit prospective acceptable representation. Uppercase WASHINGTON is a source-literal capital location and is not expanded to Washington, D.C.

Independent AI review, before outputs, identified four additional finite interpretation choices. `West` in science06 may name the geographic region, so the approved alternative adds that literal location. `Boeing` in transport04 may reference the manufacturer within the aircraft identifier, so the alternative adds that literal organization. `HackerOne` in software04 may name the platform or the company, so the alternative omits that organization while preserving OpenJS Foundation. The qualified MPTC team in standards03 may be one full span including `team`, one span without the generic descriptor, or the complete named body plus its separately written MPTC alias. All three complete alternative tag sets still contain the standalone NIST mention. Arbitrary shortened team names are not accepted. These are scope/boundary choices, not fabricated facts. The complete airport remains one location without nested Louis Armstrong or New Orleans tags.

All seven finite representation records are in `acceptable-tag-representations.json`. Strict display scores remain separately visible. No representation is added or changed after outputs.

Source agencies may qualify attribution/ownership, but no outside facts or surrounding text are supplied to either model. Independent review accepted CAISI organization typing as institutional benchmark attribution and the MPTC hosting team as a named organizational unit; it did not invent expanded names. The same plain labels reach the native model; no hidden separate descriptions are substituted. Luna receives the short shared task policy, but no gold, source IDs, passage-coverage rationale or example answers.

Primary quality is document/source-family-equal typed literal tag-set F1, with the best only among the prospectively fixed complete acceptable tag sets. Empty/empty scores 1; either side alone empty scores 0. All 24 planned sources stay, including failed or unattempted calls as score0. Also report strict display F1, pooled tag precision/recall, entity-bearing mean F1, every empty-source false tag, exact-document correctness and native position/type span diagnostics. Do not market a valid alternate segmentation as a wrong fact.

The engineering useful-fit gate requires pooled emitted-tag precision at least0.90, entity-bearing source-equal F1 at least0.80 and at least12 entity-bearing sources. All24 planned native rows must also have complete known successful evidence; an unknown or unattempted empty case cannot be treated as demonstrated empty-case correctness. This is a point gate on the declared frame, not a population noninferiority conclusion. Any uncertainty uses source family as the paired independent unit. Mention counts are not the sample size. No extra sources, altered gold, thresholds or candidates follow a failed gate.
